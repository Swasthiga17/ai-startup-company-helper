# IdeaExecutor AI — Private Beta Founder Onboarding & Evaluation Guide (Phase 23)

Welcome to the **IdeaExecutor AI Private Beta Cohort**.

IdeaExecutor AI is an AI-powered startup operating system designed to take you from a raw problem or concept to a validated, execution-ready startup roadmap with multi-agent intelligence.

---

## 🎯 Private Beta Objective
To rigorously evaluate whether IdeaExecutor AI saves founders meaningful time and delivers concrete, trustworthy insights for building their startups.

---

## 🧭 Founder Onboarding Journey (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1: Create Your Account & Register Startup
1. Navigate to the deployed app (e.g. `https://ideaexecutor.onrender.com` or your local instance `http://localhost:5173`).
2. Click **Get Started** or **Register** to create your founder profile.
3. Enter your startup's core premise:
   * **Problem**: What pain point are you solving?
   * **Target Audience**: Who experiences this pain most intensely?
   * **Initial Solution Idea**: Your product/service approach.
   * **Monetization & Industry**: Target industry vertical and business model.

### Step 2: Multi-Agent Deep Dive Execution
Click **Generate Startup Blueprint** to orchestrate the AI Agent suite:
* 🔍 **Market Research Agent**: Validates TAM/SAM/SOM, market drivers, and trends.
* ⚔️ **Competitor Intelligence Agent**: Maps direct/indirect competitors, gaps, and differentiation.
* 🛡️ **SWOT & Devil's Advocate Agent**: Challenges your assumptions and identifies blind spots.
* 📦 **MVP Roadmap Planner**: Generates phased development sprints.
* 💰 **Revenue & Unit Economics Forecaster**: Calculates 3-year projections, CAC, and LTV.
* 📋 **Action Items Kanban Workspace**: Generates prioritized execution tickets.

### Step 3: Test Real-World Features
* 🧠 **Live Intelligence & Change Detection**: Review market signals.
* ⚖️ **Founder Decision Center**: Test trade-off simulator and log critical startup choices.
* 📄 **Document Generator**: Export your Pitch Deck & Executive One-Pager.

## 📊 9-Point Founder Evaluation Rubric

During your trial, please rate each dimension on a scale of **1 to 5** (1 = Poor / Generic, 5 = Exceptional / Actionable):

| # | Evaluation Dimension | Key Question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **AI Output Quality** | Are the outputs specific and nuanced, or generic fluff? |
| 2 | **Market Research Value** | Did it uncover trends, metrics, or insights you didn't know? |
| 3 | **Competitor Analysis** | Are competitor listings accurate and their weaknesses spot-on? |
| 4 | **SWOT & Business Model** | Did the Devil's Advocate challenge your real blind spots? |
| 5 | **MVP Roadmap Actionability** | Can your tech team immediately build the proposed sprint items? |
| 6 | **Revenue & Unit Economics** | Are the pricing tiers, margins, and financial logic sensible? |
| 7 | **Agent Speed & Responsiveness** | Did multi-agent synthesis complete within acceptable time? |
| 8 | **UI/UX & Workflow Clarity** | Was the interface intuitive, responsive, and seamless? |
| 9 | **Overall Founder ROI** | Would you pay ₹999/mo or $29/mo for continuous AI co-founder guidance? |
